## `/projects/sol` — process cards

Shared direction: premium editorial technical illustration, dark graphite background, warm ivory linework, one restrained amber signal, precise Solana data-infrastructure aesthetic, shallow isometric depth, subtle glass and metal materials, generous negative space, no neon cyberpunk, no coins, no crypto logos, no text, no watermark, transparent background.

### Resolve

Use case: stylized-concept. A raw Solana wallet address entering a precise resolver instrument and emerging as a structured mint identity graph; one amber pulse travels from input to resolved entity; compact horizontal composition for a process card.

### Collect

Use case: stylized-concept. Three distinct evidence streams — on-chain transactions, market liquidity and public cultural signals — converging into one ordered evidence bundle; visible provenance paths; compact horizontal composition for a process card.

### Explain

Use case: stylized-concept. A restrained AI synthesis instrument arranging fragmented evidence into a short layered explanation; observed facts and inference remain visually separated; no humanoid robot or brain cliché; compact horizontal composition for a process card.

### Verify

Use case: stylized-concept. Four claims connected one-to-one to source records, with three verified links and one deliberately unresolved unknown; confidence is conveyed structurally, not with a shield or lock cliché; compact horizontal composition for a process card.

Animation intent after generation: use a masked amber route travelling through each still, 2.4–3.2 seconds, low amplitude, pause on hover, disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
